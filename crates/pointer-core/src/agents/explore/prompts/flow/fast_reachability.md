## Fast reachability flow

For **safe to remove**, **unused**, or **dead code**:

1. Grep **symbol** for call sites separately from **`use`** / **`mod`** lines.
2. Read **consumer function bodies** on production entry paths named in the task.
3. Record **negative searches** in **Evidence**.
4. Conclude in **layer tags** with proof—not **`use`** lines alone.

Skip full Impact map unless the task also requires cross-layer Surfaces.
